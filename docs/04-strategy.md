# Business model & product roadmap

## Proposed business model
- **Primary audience:** Vietnamese public-school students aged 11–18.
- **Economic buyer:** parents.
- **Future channels:** direct-to-consumer subscriptions, schools and learning partners.
- **Proposed prices:** 299,000 VND/month; 1,499,000 VND/year; 3,999,000 VND lifetime.

These are proposed prices rather than evidence of paid conversion or earned revenue.

## Proposed roadmap
| Stage | Focus | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MVP & pilot | Quiz game, learning concepts, user testing | Pilot conducted |
| Beta | Refine onboarding, test new content and UX | Proposed |
| Initial launch | Distribution and user acquisition | Proposed |
| Product growth | Learning path, content expansion and gamification | Proposed |
| Expansion | Parent experience, analytics and school partnerships | Proposed |

The source presentation includes a timeline, but dates are planning assumptions and may have changed.

## Strategic questions
- Is learning engagement sustained beyond first use?
- Which learning formats translate to demonstrable skill gains?
- How will parents evaluate value and willingness to pay?
- Is school distribution viable relative to acquisition costs?

## Success metrics to validate next
Activation, module completion, repeat learning behavior, user-rated clarity, knowledge gain and parent-perceived usefulness.
