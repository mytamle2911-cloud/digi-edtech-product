# MVP validation & product insights

## Sample accounting

**Confirmed complete pilot totals:** 52 students and 15 parents (confirmed by the project owner).

**Subset in the supplied presentation:** 36 students and 14 parents.

These are different denominators. Until the updated participant-level summary is supplied, this document does not calculate or rebase percentages for the complete sample.

## Validation approach (source proposal)
- Let students try the MVP, including quiz and learning modules.
- Observe behavior during use.
- Collect post-experience feedback from students and parents.
- Examine engagement, usability and perceived usefulness.

## Reported subset measurements (not full-pilot estimates)

For the **36-student subset**, the source presentation reported:
- 88.9% liked game quizzes.
- 83.3% found content easier to understand than conventional learning.
- 77.8% completed one module.
- 72.2% reported learning more about AI.

For the **14-parent subset**, the source presentation reported:
- 85.7% considered the content suitable.
- 64.3% were willing to let their child keep using DIGI after completion.

Do not describe these rates as results for all 52 students or all 15 parents.

## Feedback-to-action mapping

| Feedback theme | Proposed product response |
| --- | --- |
| Unsure how to begin | Introduce lightweight onboarding |
| Initial interest but weak repeat use | Short daily learning tasks |
| Completion does not show skill gain | Outcome-focused progress feedback |
| Parent cannot connect content to school/life | Explain real-world learning benefits |
